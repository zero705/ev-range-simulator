## [0.1.0] - 2026-09-28

### Added

- Verified data for four cars (Tesla Model 3 RWD, Volvo EX30, Polestar 2, Volkswagen ID.4
  Pro): EU WLTP registrations 2024 from the EEA, EPA coastdown road load, and the battery and
  mains energy of each car's EPA certification test, read from the EPA certificate summaries.
- Drive cycles (WLTC class 3b and its phases, UDDS, HWFET), each compared second by second
  with the table in its regulation by a script that reads fingerprinted copies of the GTR and
  the eCFR.
- Physics model with an integration step that conserves kinetic energy exactly, and an energy
  balance from the wheels to the battery and the socket that closes exactly.
- Identification of each car's drivetrain efficiency and regeneration coefficient from its two
  EPA battery-side measurements, refused if the result is not physically possible.
- Validation against the EPA measurements the identification did not use (other wheels, a
  steady 65 mph) and against the EU WLTP consumption and range, with the charging efficiency
  the EU figures imply, a sensitivity study of every modelling choice, and a summary of the
  model's accuracy against all of these measurements in the README.
- Streamlit app: any cycle or a steady speed, extra load and climate-control power, energy
  breakdown, speed sweep, every input of each car, and every validation result.
- Scripts that build the data from the official sources and check every quoted value against
  its fingerprinted document, one that runs every check and summarises it, and one that draws
  the README figures in a light and a dark version.
- Regulatory constants (reference air, driver mass, rotating mass) and unit conversions tied by
  tests to verbatim quotes from UN GTR No. 15, Regulation (EU) 2017/1151, 40 CFR 1066.305 and
  NIST SP 811.
- Documentation of the method and of every data source; the tables in the documents are
  generated by the code, and tests check the numbers in the text against the data and the
  model, so neither can drift.
- Test suite with 100 % coverage of the package, tests of the app, and continuous integration
  on Linux and Windows with Python 3.10 to 3.14, a test of the app with its pinned deployment
  requirements, a monthly re-check of the EPA certificates and the regulations at their
  source, a dependency audit and monthly Dependabot updates.

### Security

- The app shows visitors no error details, runs without Streamlit's "magic", and states its
  cross-origin and request-forgery protections explicitly.
- Downloads by the tools are HTTPS-only (redirects included) and size-capped. A document takes
  its place only if its fingerprint matches, and is parsed only after that check; spreadsheets
  are parsed with defusedxml.
- The app's deployment requirements are pinned and tested as pinned.
- The GitHub Actions are pinned to commit SHAs. The workflows are read-only and keep no
  credentials, Dependabot waits a week before proposing a release, and CodeQL and the
  flake8-bandit rules check the code; see SECURITY.md.
